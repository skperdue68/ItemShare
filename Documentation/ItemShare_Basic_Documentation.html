ItemShare - ESO Addon

Overview

ItemShare is an addon that helps you keep track of items you are willing to share with others.

On its own, the addon has a very limited role. It simply collects item data and stores it in your SavedVariables. That data is then uploaded to the ItemShare website, where it becomes a full item-sharing system. The website is a separate project; this repository contains the addon only.

The site allows you and others to:

  • See what items are available
  • Browse and search items easily
  • Mark items you would like to receive
  • Coordinate sharing without using guild banks or chat spam

Why Use ItemShare?

  • Avoid spamming guild chat with item lists
  • Share items without relying on guild bank access
  • Keep a clean, organized list of what you have available

Requirements

  • LibCustomMenu (required)
  • Elder Scrolls Online (current version)

Quick Start

  1. Right-click an item in your inventory and choose Add to Share
  2. Repeat for any items you want to share
  3. Run /ishare save or reload your UI
  4. Upload your SavedVariables file to the ItemShare website
  5. Use the site to manage requests

What the Addon Does

  • Adds right-click options to inventory items so you can add or remove them from your shared list
  • Refuses items that are bound to you, and items the game only lets you trade to your group for a short while
  • Shows a badge on items that are shared, always shared, or keep a reserve
  • Keeps track of item counts and where they are stored
  • Provides a window (/ishare list) showing your shared items
  • Saves your data so it can be uploaded to the site

Badges on the Item Icon

Hovering a badge explains which state the item is in, and how much is being kept back when it has a reserve.

  • Green gift - shared right now
  • Gold gift with a closed ring - always shared; it stays on the item even when you hold none
  • Amber padlock tag - added to either badge when the item keeps a share reserve
  • Padlock on its own - a reserve left on an item that is not shared at all, so it is still visible and can be cleared

List Window

The Shared Items window shows everything currently offered. Only rows that offer something are listed.

  • Right-click a row to stop sharing it, or to stop the standing offer it stands for
  • Left-click the item name to view full item details
  • A row with a reserve says keep N beside its location

Always Sharing an Item

Right-click an item and choose Always Share This Item to make it a standing offer. It stays offered even when you no longer hold a copy, and /ishare sync never removes it. A normal share is different: it is only offered while it is in a bag the addon can read.

Share Reserve

A share reserve keeps some of a stack back and offers only the quantity above it. The list recalculates whenever your inventory changes, so a share that is inside its reserve comes back on its own when you acquire more.

Setting a reserve from the item's right-click menu

You do not have to type a command. Right-click the item and choose Share Reserve: a small window opens with the item already named and asks "Keep how many back?". Type the number and choose Keep This Many.

Once a reserve is set, that same menu offers Share Reserve: Keep N Back... to change the number and Clear Share Reserve to remove it. This works wherever the item's right-click menu appears: your inventory, the bank, house storage and the furnishing vault. The reserve belongs to the item rather than to one copy, so it stays with the item when that copy leaves your bags.

A number that is not a whole number of at least 1 is refused instead of being saved. A reserve is only offered while you hold more than that number: keep 12 and hold 20, and 8 are shared; use down to 10 and nothing is shared until you pick more up.

Commands

CommandDescription
/itemshareShows the command list
/itemshare listOpen or close the shared items window
/itemshare reset (or clear)Clear every share and every standing offer
/itemshare sync (or cleanup, reconcile)Recount shares against the bags that are readable now and drop the ones you no longer have
/itemshare rebuildRewrite every share from your current bags; also runs on login and when a bank opens
/itemshare saveRebuild, then offer to reload the UI so the file is written
/itemshare debugToggle status messages on or off
/itemshare guildsList the guilds captured for the current character
/itemshare alwaysList the items you always offer
/itemshare noalways <name>Stop always sharing one item
/itemshare reserve <amount> <name>Keep that many back and offer the rest
/itemshare noreserve <name>Share the whole stack again
/itemshare reservesList every item with a reserve, and what it offers
/itemshare stampRefresh the guild list that shares are matched against
/itemshare exportWrite a website-compatible copy of your shares to ItemShareExport.lua for upload
/itemshare guildbankToggle sharing from the guild bank (off by default)

Guild names and IDs are stored once, by guild ID, and every share is matched to them; the addon no longer copies the list onto each item. Because the website reads the older layout, /itemshare export writes a complete compatibility copy to a second saved-variables file, ItemShareExport.lua. Upload that file until the site reads the guild table directly.

Alias: /ishare

Notes

  • You must reload your UI to save data before exporting
  • The addon does not upload data automatically
  • All processing happens on the ItemShare website

Known Issues

  • Guild bank sharing is off by default while it is being tested; /ishare guildbank toggles it
  • If an item is marked as shared while in the Furnishing Vault and is later placed in a house, it will continue to show as shared. This behavior may change in a future update
